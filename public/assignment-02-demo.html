02 THE IDEA

THE SAME FRAME
CAN FEEL DIFFERENT.

Colour can make a design feel premium, energetic, calm, playful or modern — without changing the core layout.

01

REBUILD

Match the original frame first. Get the structure right before experimenting.

02

CHANGE THE MOOD

Use a new colour palette to create a clear emotional direction.

03

MAKE A DECISION

Don't pick colours because they look pretty. Pick them because they communicate something.

Think like a designer: “What should the viewer feel?” comes before “Which colours look good?”

03 YOUR MISSION

YOUR JOB IS SIMPLE.
REBUILD → SWITCH.

01 / PICK

Choose ONE frame

Pick a creator frame with clear text, a background, graphic elements and roughly 3–5 main colour areas.

02 / RECREATE

Match the original

Rebuild the layout, typography, spacing, images and original colours as closely as possible.

03 / SWITCH

Create 3 moods

Keep the design mostly the same. Change the colour direction to create three different moods.

ONE DESIGN. THREE FEELINGS. Your colour choices should have a reason.

07 BUILD YOUR PALETTE

4–6 COLOURS.
ONE CLEAR MOOD.

You can use Coolors, Adobe Color, Realtime Colors, or build your own palette.

02 / BASEChoose the main background / dominant colour.
03 / CONTRASTChoose colours that make important elements stand out.
04 / ACCENTAdd one or two colours for emphasis.
05 / TESTCheck readability and balance.

08 APPLY & COMPARE

SAME DESIGN.
THREE FEELINGS.

For every version
  • Keep the layout mostly the same.
  • Keep the typography mostly the same.
  • Change the colour direction.
  • Make the mood obvious.
  • Don't add random elements.
Ask yourself
  • What should the viewer feel?
  • Does the palette support that feeling?
  • Can I still read everything?
  • Is one colour taking over?
  • Does the important information stand out?
If the mood isn't obvious without you explaining it, your colour choices probably need more work.

09 FINAL CHECK

COMPARE → FIX → EXPORT

Before you submit, compare your work side-by-side with the original reference.

Original frame was recreated accurately
All 3 moods are clearly different
Text is readable in every version
Colour choices feel intentional
The design still has a clear hierarchy
No unnecessary elements were added
Palettes have 4–6 colours
HEX codes are included
Don't polish a tiny detail while the whole frame is still off. Fix the big visual problems first.
